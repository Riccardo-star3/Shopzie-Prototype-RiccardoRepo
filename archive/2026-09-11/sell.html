Two ways to earn, one account

You do not choose once and get stuck with it. A braider who also sells hair products lists both from the same place.

Sell products

Anything you make, grow or stock. You set the price and the postage, and you post the order yourself.

  • You set your own shipping rates and delivery estimate
  • You set your own returns window, between 7 and 30 days
  • Buyers see a VAT-inclusive price; we handle the VAT

10%commission per sale

Offer a service

Braiding, tailoring, catering, trades, photography. You decide what is included and when you are free.

  • Build up to three packages, and say what each one includes
  • Ask buyers what you need to know before you start
  • Message the buyer once the booking is paid

15%commission per booking

Card and payment processingWe pay it

Commission comes out of your payout, so you never send us money. Card processing costs about 2.5 to 3 per cent and we absorb it inside the commission rather than adding it to your bill.

Getting paid

The buyer pays us, not you. Then we pay you.

We hold the money while the order is in progress, which is what makes a stranger willing to buy from someone they have never heard of. For a product, your money is released once the buyer confirms it arrived, or 14 days after tracking shows it delivered. For a service, once the buyer confirms the work is done, or after 7 days if they go quiet.

Payout schedule

  • Every Friday, for orders completed by Wednesday midnight
  • €10 minimum. Anything smaller rolls over to the following week
  • Bank transfer — SEPA in the EU, local bank in Ghana
  • Mobile money in Ghana, through MTN MoMo or Vodafone Cash

What you will need to apply

  • Your full name, date of birth and phone number
  • A home or business address
  • A government ID — passport, national ID or driving licence
  • An IBAN, or a mobile money number if you are in Ghana
  • A business name and VAT number, only if you have them

What you get

Your dashboard

  • Products and services in one place, with separate tabs if you do both
  • Orders and bookings as they come in
  • What you have earned, and what is still being held

Talking to buyers

  • Messaging before and after a purchase
  • Email and in-app alerts for new orders and messages
  • Reviews on your profile, which is how new buyers decide

Questions people ask

Can I sell products and offer services at the same time?

Yes, and most people who do both earn more. It is one application and one dashboard, with a tab for each.

Do I need a registered business?

No. Business name and VAT number are optional. You do need a government ID and a bank account or mobile money number in your own name.

Who handles VAT?

We do. VAT is worked out at checkout based on where the buyer is, collected by us, and reported on your behalf. Buyers always see a price with VAT already in it.

What if a buyer says the order never arrived?

Message them first — most things are sorted that way. If you cannot agree, either of you can open a dispute and our support team reads the order, the messages and the tracking, then decides. Tracking is what protects you, so always add it.

Where are the buyers?

Mostly Finland to begin with, then the rest of the EU. Services are bought locally, so if you offer one, buyers near you will find you first.

Ready when you are

Applying takes about ten minutes, and a person will have read it within two days.
