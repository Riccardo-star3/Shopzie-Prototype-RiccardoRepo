About this cloth

Two yards of kente woven on a narrow loom in Bonwire, in the Ashanti region. The strips are woven separately and then hand-stitched together, which is why the pattern steps slightly where they meet. That step is how you tell a woven cloth from a printed one.

Heavy enough to hold a fold, soft enough to drape. People buy this length for a stole, a wrap, or to have made into something by a tailor.

Good to know

  • Colours vary a little between batches, because the yarn is dyed in small lots
  • Wash by hand in cool water, or dry clean. Do not tumble dry
  • Every piece is cut from a continuous weave, so the pattern will not line up identically to the photograph
