Why a start plus a length, rather than two times. A range needs two numbers. Picking an end time from a second list is two scrolls and two taps, and lets you pick an end before the start. A length is one tap, it cannot be invalid, and it is how the catalogue already talks — the service beside this one is literally called Home cleaning, two hours. Lengths that would run past the provider's last hour are dimmed before you can choose them.

Why every tap toggles a day. A drag across the grid would be fewer gestures for a run, but on a phone it fights the page scroll. Tapping each day costs one tap per day, works the same for a run and for scattered days, and needs nothing explained. Consecutive days join into a band so a run looks like a run.
