Each version has its own permanent link. Once a version is published its link never changes and its contents are never edited again, so a link shared today still shows the same thing in a year, and a decision can be traced back to what people were actually looking at when they made it.

Version 10 — the seller dashboard, in panels

Published 21 September 2026

The seller dashboard now looks like the checkout: each section is one white panel, with tasks, orders and listings as rows inside it rather than loose cards. Long lists open on the five most urgent and grow ten at a time with Show more. The order filters are pill buttons with an “All” option and a count on each, so it is clear they filter the list below. Three lines of text that told a seller nothing are gone.

How this works

So that a link shared with the team keeps working for as long as anyone needs it:

  • Every version gets its own dated folder, and that becomes its permanent link.
  • A published version is never edited. Corrections go into the next version, not backwards into an old one.
  • This page is the only link that changes. It gains a row each time, and the rows already on it stay exactly as they are.

Share a version link when you want someone to look at a specific version. Share this page when you want them to find whichever is current.
