# What changed — version 18, 15 October 2026

The wishlist comparison, simplified. Version 17 went out earlier the same day; this
replaces the lower half of that page and leaves everything else alone.

Everything here is still placeholder content. **Nothing is a commitment.**

Earlier notes: [version 17](../v17-2026-10-15/WHAT-CHANGED.md) · [version 16](../v16-2026-10-14/WHAT-CHANGED.md) · [version 15](../v15-2026-10-14/WHAT-CHANGED.md)

## Everything you saved is in the comparison

Version 17 compared three products at a time and listed the rest underneath so you could
swap one in. With three or fewer products saved, that lower section showed exactly the same
products as the comparison directly above it — the page said everything twice.

The limit was what forced that. If only three of five can be columns, the other two need
somewhere to live, and that somewhere ends up being a second list of the same things.

**So there is no limit now.** Every product you have saved is a column. The table scrolls
sideways when there are more than fit, and the labels down the left stay put while it does.
No picking, no swapping, nothing to set up.

The trade, said plainly: with six saved you cannot see the first and the last at the same
time. That is the cost of a page with one idea on it instead of three.

## Remove something and it waits at the bottom

**Taken off your wishlist** is the new section underneath, and it only appears when there
is something in it. Remove a product and it drops there with one button — **Put it back**.

Before this, removing something meant going back to the shop, finding the product again and
saving it again. Remove sits next to Move to basket in every column, so it needed an undo.

It is not a second wishlist. It holds what you took off, it clears when you close the tab
along with the list itself, and an item disappears from it the moment you save that product
again anywhere. **Moving something to your basket does not put it there** — that is not
losing it.

## Remove looks like a button

In each column, **Remove** now sits beside **Move to basket** as a matching outlined button
rather than a plain word. It is grey until you point at it and turns red then, so a
destructive action stays quiet until you mean it.

## Why this is version 18 and not a fix to version 17

Version 17 was already published when this work started. The rule in `RELEASING.md` is that
a published folder is never edited again — not for a typo, not for one button — because a
link shared today has to show the same thing in a year. So version 17 stays exactly as it
was sent, and this is version 18.

## Known gaps, so nobody has to find them

- **A seller has no page of their own.** "Visit the shop" still goes nowhere.
- **Messaging has no screen.** The Message button in a booking does nothing.
- **Nothing anywhere mentions import VAT or customs duty**, and goods ship from Ghana into
  the EU.
- **There is still no logo file.** The wordmark is live text.
- A wishlist is not remembered between visits, as the page itself says.
